The New Highschool


The Why Behind It



    My town currently has one public high school, Hudson Senior High School. 
However, that is about to change. For the first time in 57 years, a Catholic High School 
will be opened in my county. The purpose is to form students in the Catholic Faith.
And the high school will be named Solanus Catholic High School,in honor of Blessed Solanus Casey.



Who are the teachers?



    A lot of parents want to know who the teachers will be before they send 
their kids to the school. Mrs. Joany said to a reporter, I can't send my 
daughter here when I don't even know who the principal is!Some parents 
share this opinion, but others are excited by the chance for children to attend a Catholic School.



Who are the students?



    I don't personally know any of the new students, but the first 
class will only be nine students. Some students are likely from 
St. Patrick Catholic School, which is a k-8 program and the temporary 
location of the new high school. With such a small class, students will get 
personalized attention from teachers.
